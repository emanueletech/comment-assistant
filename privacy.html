Who uses it

Emanuele Tech Commenti is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel @emanuele_tech and the Instagram account @emanuele_tech and of the Emanuele_tech models on MakerWorld, to read and reply to comments posted on his own videos, posts and models. It is not offered to other users.

What data it uses

  • Comments on the owner's content: comment text, the commenter's public display name and ID, and the title and description of the video or post, read through the YouTube Data API and the Instagram API. On MakerWorld, comments and print reviews are read in the owner's own browser, and replies are sent only when he presses Send.
  • The owner's authorization: OAuth tokens that let the tool reply on the owner's behalf.

How it is used

  • Comments are used only to draft and post replies on the owner's channel and account.
  • To draft a reply, the comment and the title and description of the content are sent to Anthropic's Claude API. Drafts that need approval reach the owner through a private Telegram bot.
  • The tool keeps only the IDs of the comments it has already handled (for 60 days), the drafts waiting for approval and a short log for the daily summary (7 days), on the owner's private server.
  • No data is sold, used for advertising, or shared with anyone else.

Revoking access

Access to the YouTube channel can be revoked at any time from Google Account → Third-party connections, and access to the Instagram account from Instagram → Settings → Website permissions → Apps and websites.

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
